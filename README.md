
# Packzy — Shopify E-commerce Website

**WordPress to Shopify Migration | Custom Shopify Theme Development | UI/UX Redesign**

A modern e-commerce storefront for packaging products, rebuilt on **Shopify** from an existing **WordPress/WooCommerce** website. The redevelopment focuses on clearer product discovery, a consistent shopping experience, responsive design, and an interactive custom-packaging enquiry workflow.

🌐 **Live website:** [packzy.com.pk](https://packzy.com.pk/)  
👨‍💻 **Developer:** [Muhammad Aliakber](https://github.com/mrajdeveloper)

## Project Overview

**Packzy** sells packaging for bakeries, gifting, small businesses, and branded products. The project involved redesigning the storefront and transitioning the online store from WordPress/WooCommerce to Shopify.

The aim was to create a polished, easy-to-navigate shopping experience that works well across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices, while adding helpful tools for customers who need ready-made or custom-branded packaging.

## Key Features

### 1. Custom Shopify Storefront

- Redesigned homepage with branded imagery and product highlights
- Custom collection showcases and product cards
- Clear calls to action and consistent visual styling
- Product detail pages designed for easier browsing

### 2. Collections & Product Discovery

- Organized packaging categories, including bakery boxes, transparent PVC boxes, and other packaging
- Dedicated collection and product browsing experiences
- Desktop category navigation and mobile-friendly menus
- **Packaging finder** with product-type and measurement inputs to help customers explore suitable products
- **Saved packaging selections** for revisiting products in the same browser

### 3. Interactive Custom Packaging & Branding

A custom enquiry flow helps customers communicate their packaging requirements:

- Enter box **length, width, and height**
- Select packaging style, material or finish, and quantity
- Preview the concept using customer-provided measurements
- Select a **print face** (such as front, back, left, or right)
- View a rotating packaging concept for the selected print placement
- Enter an optional **HEX colour code** for brand text
- Preview a logo and provide an artwork link
- Send a custom packaging quotation enquiry

*The visual preview is a concept, not a production-ready proof. Final materials, specifications, pricing, and feasibility are confirmed separately.*

### 4. Responsive Mobile Experience

- Adapted layouts for phones, tablets, and desktops
- Mobile bottom navigation for essential shopping destinations
- Touch-friendly product cards, menus, and actions
- Improved spacing, text readability, and image presentation

### 5. E-commerce Experience

- Shopify-based storefront and shopping bag
- Product options and collection browsing
- Customer account and contact entry points
- Integrated product discovery and custom-packaging enquiry journeys
